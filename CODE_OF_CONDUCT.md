# Code of Conduct

Godstack adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, critique the work and never the person, and keep the jokes aimed at committees, not at people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits and pull requests that don't follow this code, and may block anyone who repeatedly or seriously violates it. Comments from people outside the project may be moderated.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the email address on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
