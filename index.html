The law of unintended inheritance

Many lives make a shared environment. Something new learns to live there. It inherits a problem, not necessarily its makers’ purpose. This is the story’s organizing idea, not a law of nature.

Resemblance is not equivalence

Nested structure can be a powerful analogy. Atoms are quantum systems, not tiny solar systems. Galaxies are bound by their distributed mass, not generally by their central black hole alone. Neither the Planck scale nor a mathematical limit proves that the arrow of time disappears. Infinite self-similar reality is the fictional leap here.
