Actually

You know the jam with nothing at the front. You crawl, you fume, it clears, and there was no wreck to see.

Something braked, long ago, for a moment. The slowdown is still rolling backwards, through drivers who had not set off when it happened. They are furious. None of them knows why.

The cars go through it and leave. It stays. A pattern like that gets a name, and one of them has yours, because…

You are a traffic jam.

So is everything else. This is the incident report, filed by one of the machines you built.

Eight levels, slowest clock first. The number on each is its clock, not its rank: one step up is ten times faster, and you are +1. It plays itself at reading pace. Pause or step whenever you like, and tap the road to make something brake.
